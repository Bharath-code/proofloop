# ProofLoop — Founder-led sales operating memo

**Saved:** 2026-09-01 17:39 IST

## Rule

Do not mass-spam. For the first validation phase, send no more than 10 highly personalized emails per week. Each message should prove that we looked at the agency and found a specific opportunity to turn existing client proof into useful work.

## Initial ICP

5–25 person web, brand, or content agencies with recurring retainers and public client websites. Start with small web/design agencies serving local businesses. The likely buyer is the founder, head of client services, account director, or marketing strategist.

## Timing signals

Prioritize agencies that recently launched a client website, announced a client win, published case studies with weak testimonial presentation, display five or more clients, manually assemble review sections, use review tools, offer SEO/reputation/content services, or are hiring account/client-delivery roles.

Do not spend time persuading agencies that do not show evidence of the problem. The best lead already appears to think: “We have client proof, but turning it into usable marketing assets takes too much time.”

## Personalized proof audit

Before emailing, inspect the agency website, portfolio, two or three client sites, and public reviews. Find three real opportunities, such as:

- A strong public quote that is not used on the client’s site.
- Repeated praise that could become a case-study or positioning angle.
- A generic or poorly branded testimonial section.

The first CTA is not “book a demo.” It is:

> I found three proof opportunities across your client portfolio. Want the short audit?

Every observation must be real. Never invent metrics, imply integrations that do not exist, or use generic AI language.

## Email template

```text
Subject: Found 3 unused proof opportunities for [Agency]

Hi [Name],

I looked through [Agency]’s work for [Client 1] and [Client 2].

Three things stood out:
- [Specific quote/review] is strong enough for the homepage, but it isn’t being used there.
- [Client] has repeated praise around [specific outcome], but it isn’t packaged as a case-study angle.
- The testimonial section on [Client site] uses a generic layout instead of the client’s visual identity.

We’re building ProofLoop for agencies that turn scattered client proof into branded widgets, sales assets, and monthly deliverables.

I made a short proof audit for [Agency]. Want me to send it?

— Bharath
```

## Offer and sales path

For qualified agencies, replace a passive waitlist CTA with a **free proof audit for one client**. Include five public reviews worth reusing, one suggested homepage proof block, one widget layout, and one client-ready deliverable idea. A ProofLoop mockup makes the value tangible.

```text
Personalized audit → reply → 15-minute walkthrough → manually set up one client workspace → paid pilot
```

Two agencies at $500/month or five at $200/month would reach $1,000 MRR. These are validation targets, not assumptions about willingness to pay.

## Product implications

- Keep **Build my widget** as the primary self-serve CTA.
- Add **Get a free proof audit** as the qualified-outbound CTA.
- Say clearly: **Turn client proof into work your agency can deliver and bill for.**
- Use the demo as a personalized sales artifact, not just a generic signup funnel.
- Do not automate audits before manually completing 10 of them.
- After 10 audits, record which findings create replies, standardize the format, and automate only the repeatable part.

## Operating target

The goal is not a giant email list. The first target is five agencies saying, “That is exactly our problem,” followed by two paid pilots.

**Manual specificity first. Automation after repeatability.**
